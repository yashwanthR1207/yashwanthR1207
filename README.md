## About Me

> *"Engineering is not just about solving problems — it is about building a better world."*

I am an Electrical and Electronics Engineering student at **VVCE Mysore**, passionate about designing and building real-world systems that combine hardware intelligence with modern technology.

From microcontroller programming to IoT-based automation, I enjoy working at the intersection of electronics and embedded software. I believe that every circuit tells a story — and I am here to write mine through innovation.

- Deep interest in **IoT, Sensor Systems, and Embedded C/C++**
- Hands-on experience with **ESP8266, Arduino, and circuit design**
- Passionate about **smart automation** for real-world industrial and home applications
- Currently exploring **power systems and smart grid technologies**
